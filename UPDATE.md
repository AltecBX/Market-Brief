# Scheduled update runbook

This repo is a phone market brief: https://altecbx.github.io/Market-Brief/
`index.html` never changes in a scheduled run. Live prices (websocket, with a REST refresh every 15s) and the live news wire (every 60s) run in the page.
A scheduled run rewrites `brief.json` (and installs the daily recap after each completed session with `tools/recap.py`), archives it, checks it, and pushes to `main`.

## Run times (ET, weekdays)
One scheduled task, "Market Brief updates", starts at 7:12am, 9:12am, 1:12pm, 3:12pm, 4:12pm and 8:12pm ET (cron `12 7,9,13,15,16,20 * * 1-5`). Each run is live about 15 minutes after it starts. Never pick the row from the clock yourself: run `node tools/session.js`, which applies the NYSE calendar (holidays and 1pm closes) and prints the row, whether a recap is due (`recap`), and the session date.
| Starts | Name | Lead with | anchor ("Since" basis) | base (last cash close) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7:12am | Morning | Overnight: futures, Asia close, Europe, overnight news, today's data and earnings | 4pm yesterday | Last cash close |
| 9:12am | Pre open | Futures vs base, 8:30 data result vs consensus, premarket movers, what to watch at the open | 7:00am today | Last cash close |
| 1:12pm | Midday (on 1pm half days this run is the Close) | What the morning did and why, leaders and laggards, levels hit | 9:30am open | Last cash close |
| 3:12pm | Afternoon (on half days: Evening) | Afternoon moves, setup into the close | 12:00pm | Last cash close |
| 4:12pm | Close (on half days: Evening) | Close brief and the daily recap, about 12 minutes after the 4pm close | 9:30am open | Today's close |
| 8:12pm | Evening | After hours earnings, futures open at 6pm, Asia open at 7pm. Publishes the day's recap if the Close run did not | 4pm today | Today's close |

On market holidays there is no session and no recap: the Recap tab keeps the last completed session's recap. At the Close run, Unusual Whales end of day option levels may not be final: if they still show the prior session, keep the previous `opt` block and its `asof`.

## Steps
1. Clone: `git clone --depth 1 https://github.com/AltecBX/market-brief` and work in it.
2. Gather. Verify every number against a source, never from memory. Record each source in `sources`.
   - Index closes, futures, Asia and Europe, yields, oil, gold, VIX, Fed odds: web search (Reuters, AP, CNBC, Bloomberg, MarketWatch, Barchart, Cboe, CME FedWatch).
   - Cash closes for board stocks and ETFs: Unusual Whales `get_ticker_ohlc_latest_or_date` with `limit` 1 (field `close`, check `date` is the session you mean) for NVDA AMD MU SNDK WDC AVGO TSM ASML LRCX AMAT SMH ORCL AAPL MSFT GOOGL AMZN META TSLA. Index closes (SPX, NDX) from Cboe, Nasdaq or a news wire. Unusual Whales has no index or VIX prices.
   - Perps: `python3 tools/prices.py verify` (lists missing or delisted instruments), `python3 tools/prices.py now`, and `python3 tools/prices.py at "<ISO time with offset>"` for the perp price at an exact time. Times need the UTC offset: 16:00 ET is `-05:00` in winter and `-04:00` in summer.
   - Options (Unusual Whales tools): `get_gex_levels` SPX with source `oi` and `vol`; `get_greek_exposure_by_strike` SPX (the 10 strikes with the largest abs(call_gex)+abs(put_gex) within about 4% of spot, as [strike, call_gex, put_gex]); `get_max_pain` SPX (nearest monthly); `get_implied_volatility_term_structure` for SPXW (daily and weekly expiries), SPX (the standard monthly) and SPY. Use `implied_move_perc` x 100.
   - VIX: VIX9D, VIX, VIX3M index closes and the first 7 monthly VIX futures settlements (Cboe, vixcentral, barchart). If not found, keep the previous values and their `asof`.
   - If Unusual Whales tools are not available, keep the previous `opt` block unchanged and leave its `asof` as is.
3. News filter for `picks` (6 to 14 items, newest first, last 24h): only items that moved or can move a major market: Fed and economic data, tariffs and policy, oil and conflict, megacaps and chips (memory, storage, equipment, export rules, hyperscaler capex), index level moves, big earnings. Drop stock picks, analyst price targets, law firm alerts, crypto chatter. Each item: `[time ET, CAT, dir, text]`, CAT in FUT, ASIA, AI, OIL, MACRO, FED, EARN; dir `up`, `dn` or `""` (effect on stocks). Say whether news is new or already known. Never state a cause for a move unless a source states it.
4. Write `brief.json` (keep every existing key; `tools/check.py` lists what is required):
   - `updated` ISO time with offset, `updatedLabel` like "Fri 7:02am ET", `next` like "9:02am".
   - `kicker`, `headline` (the finding in one line), `lede` (one or two sentences), `pills` 4 to 6 `[g|r|a|b, text]`.
   - `daily` (required every run; the page opens with it): `{asofTs, happened, cause, causeSrc, since, expected, affected, priced, catalysts, invalid}`. One or two plain sentences each, numbers included, every claim sourced in `sources`.
     - `happened`: what markets did since the row's anchor. `cause`: what a source says caused it, with `causeSrc` (https link); write "not established" and leave `causeSrc` empty when no source states a cause.
     - `since`: what changed since the previous brief. Read the current brief.json before rewriting it and name what is new, what reversed and what was confirmed.
     - `expected`: `[[item, expected, actual, https source]]` for data, earnings and events released since the previous brief (consensus vs actual). Empty only when nothing was released, and then say so in `since`.
     - `affected`: 2 to 6 `[stock or sector, up|down|mixed, why]`, chips first when they moved.
     - `priced`: what options and futures price now (nearest implied move, Fed odds), consistent with `priced`.
     - `catalysts`: the 3 most important upcoming events `[when ET, what, why it matters]`.
     - `invalid`: the level or event that would invalidate the current outlook.
   - Material developments: in `stories`, set `material: true` only for developments that change earnings, rates, policy or positioning enough to matter for the index or for chips (Fed and data surprises, export rules, hyperscaler capex changes, guidance from the largest chip names, oil or conflict shocks), and add `changes`: one line on whether and how it changes the outlook. A routine headline is not material. If a material development contradicts the previous brief's outlook, say so first in `daily.since`.
   - `gaps`: `[[block, reason]]` for any block you could not fill with sourced, current data this run (for example `["priced", "CME FedWatch unreachable"]`). From the 9:02am run on Nov 27 2026, `tools/check.py` rejects a missing or empty `daily`, `top3`, `risks`, `scenarios`, `events7`, `priced`, `stories`, `semis`, `forecast` or `qa` unless it is listed here. Never fill a block with placeholder text.
   - `top3`: exactly 3 `[title, text]`, the three most important developments right now.
   - `drivers`: exactly 4 `[g|r|a, TITLE IN CAPS, text]`.
   - `risks`: 2 to 5 `[when or level, text]`, immediate risks. `calendar`: `[time, text]` for the next items, with consensus and prior for data releases, and actual vs consensus once released.
   - `scenarios`: exactly 3 objects in this order, `n` Bullish, Neutral, Bearish, each with `cond` (what must happen, with levels), `confirm` (signals that confirm), `risk`, `inval` (level or event that invalidates). No probabilities.
   - `events7`: the next 7 trading days, `[ISO date, time, text, importance 1 to 3, tag, forecast, prior, actual]` for data releases, Fed decisions and speakers, Treasury auctions and major earnings (chips and megacaps first). Holidays, half days and option expirations are computed by the page, do not add them. Fill `actual` once released.
   - `priced`: `{asof, fed: [[meeting, text, exp|conf|spec]], data: [[release, when, forecast, prior, actual, surprise text]], earn: [[ticker, when, implied move %, note]], other: [[label, text, exp|conf|spec]]}`. `exp` is a market expectation with its source (CME FedWatch, consensus), `conf` a released result, `spec` speculation. Earnings implied moves come from Unusual Whales.
   - `qa`: 6 to 12 `[question, answer, https source]` for the questions a trader gets asked now: why the market moved, why it reversed, the Fed, yields, Korea and Japan, memory prices, the biggest risks this week, what options imply, what changed since yesterday. Two sentences each, sourced; say "not established" when no source explains a move.
   - `internals`: `[label, value, source, asofTs]` such as NYSE advancers vs decliners, new highs vs lows, % of S&P stocks above the 50 and 200 day averages (WSJ market data, Barchart, StockCharts). Required on the 12:02pm, 3:02pm and 4:02pm rows; before the open keep the previous close's readings with their own time, or list `internals` in `gaps`.
   - `forecast`: `{dir: "up" or "down", date: ISO date of the session it is about, ref: SPX level it is measured against, regime: the regime label shown on the page if known, basis: one line on why}`. Make exactly one call per brief. Several briefs about the same session are scored once (the last call made before that session's close), so do not repeat a call to inflate the record.
   - Timestamps: every block carries the time its data is from, not the time of the run: `opt.asofTs` and `opt.vix.asofTs` (for example the 4:15pm close the data came from), `semis.asofTs`, `priced.asofTs`, `internals` rows `[label, value, source, asofTs]`. If you reuse older data, keep its older time; the page marks it stale rather than presenting it as current. One call per brief about the next close vs `ref`. It is scored automatically from the archive and is never edited later.
   - `stories`: 2 to 5 consequential stories, each `{t: time ET, h: short title, what, why, assets, impl: bull|bear|mixed, read, reacted, known: new|known, confirm, invalid, src: https link}`. `reacted` states the measured price move after the timestamp and whether it began before the story; never claim a move was caused by a story unless the source says so. Mark a story `known` when it repeats earlier reporting.
   - `semis`: `{asof, asofTs, memory: [[item, reading, https source]], notes: [[TOPIC, text, https source]]}`. Memory: one row each for DRAM, NAND and HBM (contract or spot price trend, quarter on quarter guidance, supply and allocation; TrendForce, DRAMeXchange, Counterpoint, company commentary). If nothing new was published, keep the latest reading and say "no new pricing since <date>" with that source. Use TOPIC tags SUPPLY, DEMAND, CAPEX (hyperscaler spending), EXPORT (restrictions and licenses), EARNINGS, GUIDANCE, PRICING, HBM, KOREA, TAIWAN, JAPAN. Notes: supply and demand, export restrictions, AI and hyperscaler spending, earnings and guidance, estimate revisions, and Korea, Taiwan and Japan chip market news, for NVDA AMD MU SNDK WDC STX AVGO TSM ASML LRCX AMAT KLAC SMH SOXX SK Hynix Samsung Kioxia. 3 to 8 notes, newest first. Skip anything you cannot source.
   - `opt.skew` (every run; list `skew` in `gaps` only if the chains cannot be pulled): `{asof, rows: [[expiry label, 25 delta put IV %, ATM IV %, 25 delta call IV %], ...]}` for the next weekly and the monthly SPX expiry, from `get_chains_for_expiry`. `opt.ivchg`: `[[label, IV % now, IV % at the previous update]]` for the same expiries.
   - `opt.zero` (9:02am, 12:02pm and 3:02pm runs on expiry days): `{asof, expiry, note, rows: [[strike, call_gex, put_gex], ...]}` for today's SPX expiry from `get_greek_exposure_by_strike_expiry`, the 8 largest strikes within 2% of spot. Leave it out when there is no same day expiry or no data.
   - `means`: 3 to 4 lines on what it means next (`<b>` allowed).
   - `base`: `{label, ts, spx, closes, perp, closesSrc, prevChg}`. `ts` is the last cash close time. `closes` are real cash closes only, keyed by perp id (xyz:SP500 = SPX, xyz:XYZ100 = NDX, xyz:NVDA = NVDA). `perp` is `prices` from `tools/prices.py at "<base.ts>"`. `prevChg` is the S&P % change of the session before `base` (number).
   - `anchor`: `{label, ts, prices}` with `prices` from `tools/prices.py at "<anchor.ts>"`.
   - `ladder`: bull trigger, range floor, 4 to 6 marks `[price, short label, 0|1]` (1 = label above), and `bullText`, `rangeText`, `riskText`.
   - `chart`: title, start (= base.ts), startLabel, marker `[ISO time, label]` for the key event.
   - `opt`: `asof`, `base` (the SPX close the options data is from), `em` rows `[label, pct, expiry note, "SPXW" or "SPX", ISO expiry date]` nearest first (next daily, next Friday weekly, standard monthly), `emSpy` rows `[label, pct, expiry note]`, `spyBase` (SPY close), `emSrc`, `vix {asof, idx: [["9D",9,x],["VIX",30,x],["3M",91,x]], fut: [[month, days, settle], ...]}`, `levels` `[name, price, r|g|a|b]`, `gex`.
   - `sources`: `[[title, https url], ...]` for what you used.
5. Close run only, history and research: run `python3 tools/history.py cboe` (Cboe and FRED). Call Unusual Whales `get_ticker_ohlc_latest_or_date` with `limit` 5 for SPY, QQQ, NVDA, MU, SNDK, AMD, RSP, SMH and IWM, save each result as JSON and run `python3 tools/history.py uw <files>`. Call `get_ticker_candles_by_range` with range 5m, interval 5d, end_interval 0d, session regular for SPY, QQQ, SMH, NVDA, MU, AMD and SNDK, save each and run `python3 tools/history.py intraday <files>` (it fills both the 5 and the 30 minute files). On Fridays also call `get_ticker_close_prices` with timeframe 3M for NVDA, MU, SMH, SOXX, QQQ, SPY, RSP, IWM, WDC and AMD and run `python3 tools/history.py uwclose <TICKER> <file>` for each. On the first close run of each month call `get_earnings_history` (quarterly) for NVDA, MU, AMD and SNDK and run `python3 tools/history.py earnings <TICKER> <file>` for each. Save option chain snapshots with `get_chains_for_expiry` (limit 1000): SPY at the expiry closest to 35 days out, and QQQ, SMH, NVDA, MU, AMD and SNDK at the weekly expiry 5 to 9 days out; save each and run `python3 tools/chains.py <file> <session date> <ISO time you pulled it>`. Then run `node tools/audit.js` and `node tools/research.js` (about 7 minutes; it also records the day's prospective predictions under data/prospective/ and writes data/research/health.json). It rewrites the result files in data/research/, adds one new file under data/research/ledger/ and never edits an earlier ledger file. Commit data/research/ and data/prospective/ including new ledger and prediction files. Never edit or delete an existing file under data/research/ledger/ or data/prospective/; tools/check.py rejects it. The tools only add or confirm rows; if one reports conflicting rows, leave them and say so in the commit message. If a download fails, skip it; the page keeps working with the older history.
6. Daily recap, only when `node tools/session.js` prints `"recap": true` and `recap.json` does not already hold that session: write `recap_draft.html` (an HTML fragment; never commit the draft) and install it with `python3 tools/recap.py install recap_draft.html --session <session> --tags "<events>"`. The installer refuses anything incomplete, unverified or unsafe, and the published recap stays as it was; fix the draft and retry, and if the data cannot be verified, do not install and say so in the commit message. Never edit `recap.html`, `recap.json` or anything under `archive/recaps/` by hand.
   - Same structure and class names as the original Wednesday Nov 25 2026 recap (`archive/recaps/2026/11/2026-11-25.html`): hero (`<section class="hero" data-session="YYYY-MM-DD">` with kicker, h1, lede, 3 or more pills, three, k), then sections with these ids: `score` (scoreboard with idx cards), `day` (how the day unfolded, with an inline `<svg>` intraday chart of the S&P from real bars), `why` (catalysts: what moved it and why, sourced), `assets` (cross asset), `rotation` (sectors and movers, with `<div id="sectors" data-sectors='[["Technology",1.2],...]'></div>` for all 11 sectors from a source), `options` (options intelligence: implied move vs the realized move, gamma levels hit, skew and IV change, 0DTE positioning, VIX and its term structure), `ask` (key questions), `next` (what is next and the market outlook, with what would invalidate it), `sources` (3 or more https links). No `<script>`, `<style>`, handlers or links other than https. On half days say so in the kicker.
   - `--tags`: the significant events of the session, from Fed, CPI, Jobs, Inflation, GDP, Tariffs, Policy, Earnings, Chips, AI, Oil, Geopolitics, Reversal, Credit, Bonds. The installer adds Selloff, Rally, VIX above 30, VIX spike, Half day and stock earnings tags from the data itself.
   - The installer writes `recap.html` (Recap tab), `recap.json` (the tab is named from it, like "Fri Recap"), a permanent standalone copy at `archive/recaps/YYYY/MM/YYYY-MM-DD.html` and the archive index entry. A correction later the same day uses `--correction "what was fixed"` and is saved as a new version; the original is kept.
7. This repo and page are public. Never write personal names, accounts, positions, API tokens or other private details into any file or commit message.
8. Style: plain English, short. No hyphens or dashes in text. Use the % symbol. Numerals for times like 4:30pm. Minus sign "−" for negatives.
9. `python3 tools/check.py` must print OK. Then `python3 tools/archive.py` (new files under `archive/` are part of every run, alongside brief.json; recaps are archived by tools/recap.py), then `python3 tools/check.py` again. Never edit or delete anything already in `archive/`.
10. Before committing run `node tests/core.test.js`, `node tests/time.test.js` and `python3 tests/check_test.py`; all must pass. Commit with a short message and push to `main` with `git -c push.negotiate=true push origin main`. Pages updates in about a minute; an open page reloads `brief.json` every 5 minutes.
11. Completeness: try to fill every optional block each run (top3, risks, scenarios, forecast, events7, priced, qa, stories, semis, internals, opt.skew, opt.ivchg, opt.zero on expiry days). For any block you could not source, leave it out rather than guessing, and list it in the commit message as `missing: <block> (<reason>)`. If a source fails, keep the last verified values for that part with their original time, push what is verified, and say what was skipped. If `check.py` cannot pass, push nothing.
