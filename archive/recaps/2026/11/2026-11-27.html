WHAT HAPPENED

Stocks opened higher, dipped to an S&P low of 7,211.46 around 10:05am, then climbed into the 1:00pm close. S&P +1.61%, Nasdaq +2.31%, Dow +1.19%. All three gained on the week.

WHY

Reuters: investors returning from Thanksgiving flocked to hard hit AI shares, and a strategist called the market oversold. Volume was thin. No source explains the last minute push.

WHAT IT MEANS

The rebound now has chip leadership that Wednesday lacked, but Nvidia did not join. The close sits just under the 7,304 gamma flip. A Dec 9 hike is still almost fully priced.

07 KEY QUESTIONS

Why did stocks jump?Reuters says investors back from Thanksgiving flocked to beaten down AI shares and one strategist called the market oversold. Volume was thin, which can exaggerate moves.
Was it broad?Yes by sector: all 11 sector ETFs rose. But the equal weight S&P (+1.22%) trailed SPY (+1.62%), so big tech led, unlike Wednesday.
Why did Nvidia lag?Not established. It opened near +2% and closed +0.80%. The FT reported Thursday that Nvidia halted AI server shipments to four Southeast Asian countries; no source ties the stock move to it.
Is the bottom in?Not confirmed. The S&P is +5.6% this week but still −3.7% for November, and the close sits just under the 7,304 gamma flip.
What about the Fed?A Dec 9 hike is almost fully priced (CME FedWatch 98.9% Friday morning). Yields rose, with the 10 year near 4.93%.
Are memory prices still a worry?Yes for 2027 and 2028: TrendForce sees NAND prices falling and flags DRAM contract decline risk. Stocks ignored it Friday.
