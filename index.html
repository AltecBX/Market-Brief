    Morning flush, noon reversal. Chips still lost.

    The S&P won back 92% of a 3.2% drop. The bounce was a rotation into cyclicals and small caps, not a return to AI leaders.

    Core PCE 3.3% hot Tariffs on 14 economies No single catalyst for the turn 74% of S&P green VIX 28.29 · peak 34.95 Dec hike odds 97.8%

    WHAT HAPPENED

    Hot inflation, a new tariff proposal and day 2 of AI selling hit at the open. S&P −3.2%, Nasdaq −4.6%, Dow −1,105 pts. Lows held through late morning, then stocks turned at about noon.

    WHY IT TURNED

    Short covering near S&P 7,000, tariff fine print softer than the headline, Burry saying he is almost entirely long, and falling yields. Thin holiday volume amplified both moves.

    WHAT IT MEANS

    Breadth was strong but leadership changed. Until NVDA, MU and the semis join, a durable bottom is unconfirmed. The Fed is still on track to hike Dec 9.

    06 KEY QUESTIONS

    Why did stocks tank Wednesday morning?Three hits at once: core PCE came in hot at 3.3%, the US proposed tariffs on 14 economies, and AI stocks kept selling off after a chip rout in Korea and Taiwan.
    What turned it around?No single headline. Shorts covered near S&P 7,000, the tariff details were softer than feared, yields fell, and Burry said he was almost entirely long. Holiday trading was thin, so the move snowballed.
    So is the bottom in?Not confirmed. 74% of stocks rose, but chips and megacap AI still closed red. Only 5 Nasdaq reversals this size since 1985, and 3 were lower a month later.
    Is the Fed cutting?No, hiking. Markets price a 25 bp hike at the Dec 8 to 9 meeting with about 97.8% odds. Inflation is 3.8% headline, nearly double target.
    Is the AI trade over?It is under pressure, not dead. NVDA is down 27% this month and the Nasdaq 100 briefly touched 20% off its record. Money moved to materials, industrials and small caps.
    How bad are the tariffs?15% for EU, Japan, Korea, Switzerland and Taiwan, up to 35% for Vietnam. They won't stack on existing duties and aren't in force. Final duties likely Feb or March.
    Where are bonds, oil, gold?10Y fell to 4.85% as a safe haven. Oil up 2% to 3.5% on a Houthi strike on a Saudi refinery. Gold down near a 14 month low.
    How was the week?Strong despite Wednesday. S&P +3.9%, Nasdaq +4.3%, Dow +4.2% in three days. The Dow closed above 50,000 for the first time Tuesday.
